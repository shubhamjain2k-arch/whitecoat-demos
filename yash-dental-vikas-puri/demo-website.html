Treatments

Braces and Aligners

Straightens teeth and corrects bite, with fixed braces or clear aligners.

Invisalign

Clear, removable aligners that straighten teeth without fixed braces.

Root Canal Treatment

Treats infection inside a tooth, to relieve pain and save the natural tooth.

Dental Implants

Replaces a missing tooth with a fixed, natural-feeling replacement.

Cleaning

Routine cleaning that removes plaque and tartar from the teeth.

Gum Treatments

Care for sore, bleeding or receding gums.

Tongue Tie

Assessment and care for a tongue-tie that affects feeding or speech.

Cosmetic Restorations and Veneers

Thin, natural-looking covers and repairs for the front of teeth.

Digital Smile Designing

A digital preview of your planned smile before treatment begins.
