A better life starts with a beautiful smile.

Dr Pahwa's Centre for Dental Excellence is a dental implant and oral healthcare clinic in Rohini, Delhi. Its team covers diagnosis of oral diseases, dental trauma management, smile designing, root canal treatment, operative dentistry and cosmetic dentistry.

Photos shown are illustrative.

Vision: to create, preserve and spread beautiful smiles.
Mission: every individual is unique, so treatment fits the person, not just the tooth.

Treatments, in plain words

Each treatment below is offered at the Rohini clinic. The short line beside it says what it is for.

01   Dental Implants
Replaces a missing tooth with a fixed, natural-feeling replacement.
02   Cosmetic Dentistry
Treatments that improve the appearance of teeth and smile.
03   Braces
Straightens teeth and corrects bite, with fixed braces or clear aligners.
04   Root Canal Treatment
Treats infection inside a tooth, to relieve pain and save the natural tooth.
05   Smile Designing
A plan for the shape, shade and alignment of your smile, agreed with you first.
06   Paediatric Dentistry
Gentle dental care for children, including check-ups and fillings.
07   Deep Cleaning and Scaling
Thorough cleaning above and below the gum line for gum health.
08   Laser Teeth Whitening
Whitening treatment that lightens tooth stains.
09   Dental Emergency
Urgent care for sudden tooth pain, swelling, or a broken tooth.
