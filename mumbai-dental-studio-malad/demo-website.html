What the studio covers

General Dentistry

Everyday dental care: check-ups, cleaning, fillings and advice.

Aesthetic Dentistry

Treatments focused on how your smile looks, planned around your teeth.

Smile Designing

A plan for the shape, shade and alignment of your smile, agreed with you first.

Paediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental care for children, including check-ups and fillings.

Dental Implants

Replaces a missing tooth with a fixed, natural-feeling replacement.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Treatments that improve the appearance of teeth and smile.

Orthodontics

Correction of crowded, spaced or misaligned teeth and bite.

Endodontics

Care for the inside of the tooth, including root canal treatment.

Periodontics

Care for gums and the bone that supports the teeth.
